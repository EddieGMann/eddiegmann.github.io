About Me

I'm a Learning Technology Program Coordinator at Duke University, where I support the use of technology in education, help faculty and instructors navigate learning platforms, and explore ways to make academic processes more efficient.

I'm a proud graduate of NC State University, where I studied French Language and Literature with a concentration in Teacher Education. Before moving into higher education technology, I spent 11 years in the classroom as a French teacher. That experience continues to shape how I approach technology: I enjoy finding practical solutions that make learning, teaching, and everyday work a little easier.

My interest in technology goes back much further than my professional career. I grew up using computers running Windows 3.1, navigating DOS to launch games, experimenting with HyperStudio, and using the green-screen computers at school. I've enjoyed watching technology evolve and finding new ways to put it to work.

Outside of work, I'm an avid reader, particularly of urban fantasy and science fiction. I also enjoy browsing cookbooks and experimenting with new recipes, even if I wouldn't necessarily call myself a cook. When I'm looking for a quieter way to spend an evening, you'll probably find me playing Stardew Valley.
